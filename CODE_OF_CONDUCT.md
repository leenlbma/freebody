# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

FreeBody 欢迎任何背景、任何训练水平的人参与。参与本项目（issue、讨论、PR、评审）即表示同意以下准则。

## 我们期望

- 尊重不同的身体条件、训练目标、饮食习惯与文化背景，不评判他人的身材与选择。
- 讨论具体问题，给出可以验证的理由；接受善意的批评，也善意地批评。
- 不确定的健康话题，说明来源与不确定性；不给他人做诊断、开药或替代就医的建议。
- 保护隐私：不公开自己或他人的真实健康记录、导出文件、照片、联系方式或 API Key。

## 不可接受

- 骚扰、人身攻击、歧视性言论，包括针对体重、外貌、性别、年龄、伤病的嘲讽。
- 发布他人的个人信息，或诱导他人提供个人健康数据。
- 推销保健品、药物或未经证实的疗法。
- 持续打断、刷屏，或在被要求停止后继续上述行为。

## 处理方式

维护者会删除、编辑或拒绝违反准则的内容，必要时暂时或永久禁止参与。请通过仓库的
[私下报告](https://github.com/leenlbma/freebody/security/advisories/new) 或在 issue 中 @ 维护者说明情况（不要在公开处转述敏感细节）。维护者会对报告人信息保密。

---

## English summary

Everyone is welcome, whatever their background or training level. By taking part in issues, discussions, pull requests or reviews you agree to:

- respect different bodies, goals, diets and cultures, and never judge someone's body or choices;
- argue about the problem with reasons that can be checked, and give and take criticism in good faith;
- state sources and uncertainty on health topics, and never diagnose, prescribe or replace medical care for others;
- protect privacy: never post your own or anyone else's real health records, backups, photos, contact details or API keys.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination (including mocking weight, appearance, gender, age or injury), doxxing, selling supplements or unproven treatments, and continued disruption after being asked to stop are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content and restrict participation. Report privately through the repository's [security advisory form](https://github.com/leenlbma/freebody/security/advisories/new) or mention a maintainer in an issue without repeating sensitive details publicly. Reporters' identities are kept confidential.
